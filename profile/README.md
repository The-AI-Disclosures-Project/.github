# AI Disclosures Project

**Open protocols, for open markets.**

The [AI Disclosures Project](https://ai-disclosures.org/) is an independent, public-interest research and prototyping initiative working to build more open, competitive, and participatory AI markets.

Led by **Tim O'Reilly and Ilan Strauss** and hosted at [Code for Science & Society](https://codeforscience.org/), we bring together researchers, technologists, policymakers, and industry participants to develop the protocols, infrastructure, and economic mechanisms needed for a healthier AI ecosystem.

## Our Mission

AI is reshaping how information is accessed, how economic value is created, and how people interact with technology. The rules governing these systems will determine whether the benefits of AI are broadly distributed or concentrated among a handful of dominant platforms.

We believe that **open protocols and meaningful disclosures are essential infrastructure for competitive AI markets.**

Our work focuses on developing the technical and institutional foundations that enable interoperability, transparency, decentralized innovation, and meaningful human participation.

## What We Do

- **Open Protocols:** Develop interoperable protocols that reduce platform lock-in and enable independent AI systems to communicate and exchange information.
- **Research:** Investigate AI market structures, transparency, attribution, data access, and the economic implications of emerging AI technologies.
- **Prototyping:** Build and evaluate technical approaches to open, decentralized AI infrastructure.
- **Convening:** Bring together researchers, developers, industry leaders, and policymakers to coordinate on shared technical and governance challenges.
- **Policy & Thought Leadership:** Translate technical findings into actionable recommendations for healthier AI markets.

## Featured Initiative

### Open Memory Protocol Initiative (OMPI)

The [Open Memory Protocol Initiative](https://github.com/The-AI-Disclosures-Project/Open-Memory-Protocol) is a collaborative effort to develop an open protocol for portable and interoperable AI-agent memory.

The initiative explores how memory can move between agents, applications, and providers without tying users to any single memory system.

Key areas include:

- Common representations for exchanging memories
- Memory identity, provenance, and lifecycle management
- Import, export, and interoperability between memory systems
- Revision, supersession, and historical context
- Extensible interfaces that accommodate different memory architectures

**[Explore the Open Memory Protocol →](https://github.com/The-AI-Disclosures-Project/Open-Memory-Protocol)**

## Research & Publications

Our research examines both the technical foundations and economic consequences of AI systems.

Selected areas of work include:

- **AI Transparency & Disclosure:** Understanding what information AI providers should disclose to enable effective oversight.
- **Training Data & Attribution:** Investigating access to copyrighted and non-public training materials, alongside attribution practices in AI-powered search.
- **AI Market Competition:** Studying platform concentration, open protocols, and emerging sources of market power.
- **Agent Infrastructure:** Examining interoperability, agent communication, memory portability, and open ecosystems.

Explore our [research and publications](https://ai-disclosures.org/research).
